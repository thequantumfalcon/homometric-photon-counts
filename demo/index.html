AN INTERACTIVE RESEARCH NOTE

Different structures.
Identical diffraction.

Two arrangements of 63 sites share every pair distance. Ordinary diffraction cannot distinguish them. Under a precise phase-noise model, the fourth moment can.

SEPARATE OPTICAL STUDY

A changed design asks a different question.

At a different frequency, a second-order signal gives a conditional 5,000-snapshot guarantee with each error below 4.53%, under the ideal optical and calibration assumptions below.

Assumptions of this separate guarantee

q = 201π/200; iid snapshots with independent Gaussian site phases and equal amplitudes; gain η ∈ [0.98, 1.02], phase variance s ∈ [0.049, 0.051], and background b ∈ [0.10, 0.15] per gate, fixed within the box. Ideal 256-level phase quantization and initially-live nonparalyzable dead time ≤22 ns per 5 ms gate are included. Afterpulsing and temporal dependence are excluded.
