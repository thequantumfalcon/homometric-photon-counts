Bounded optical robustness study
===============================

This is synthetic numerical evidence for the changed-frequency phase-mask
emulator. It does not validate the original fourth-order-only experiment,
unknown specimen imaging, continuous hardware tolerances or historical novelty.

Preservation and optics
----------------------
baseline.py is a byte-identical copy of the previous optical bench's bench.py.
Geometry, scalar Fraunhofer assumptions, wavelength, pupil layout, static
amplitude mask, photon scaling, score and threshold are unchanged. The frozen
source and input hashes are stored with each development screen and plan.
The isolated, hash-locked POPPY environment and its prior REA identity audit
are reused. REA does not perform the physical calculation or validate it.

The collected Gaussian mode is now displaced in both x and y. The continuum
coefficient integral factors into two one-dimensional Gaussian integrals with
complex quadratic and linear exponents. 16-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature
on each finite square cell evaluates them. Independent 32-point quadrature,
the old one-dimensional implementation, and POPPY adjoint propagation at
128,256,512 pupil grids check the new reduction. The optical coefficients
used in the stochastic trials are the converged continuum calculation.
POPPY supplies the separate sampled-propagation check. Neither is an interval
enclosure of the other. The blocked 64th cell and indexing are unchanged.

Correction and calibration knowledge
------------------------------------
Let c_j(delta_x,delta_y,d) be the complex collected amplitude per cell, with
signed quadratic optical-path coefficient d. The original attenuation a_j
and centered scale c_min remain fixed. Actual stochastic weights are
w_j = a_j*c_j(actual)/c_min.

The correction rule computes u_j=-arg(c_j(estimated)) and adds u_j to the
commanded phase BEFORE rounding the entire noisy phase to one of 256 levels:
theta_j = Q_256(q*x_G,j + u_j + sqrt(s)*Z_j).
Uncorrected trials set u_j=0. Counts depend on gain*|sum w_j exp(i theta_j)|^2+b.
The same correction is applied for A and B. It changes no attenuation,
photon normalization, decision score or threshold. Calibration parameters are
presumed obtained separately and are not fitted to validation labels/outcomes.

For a specified calibration residual e, estimate=actual-e. These are fixed
errors, not random extra noise. No calibration acquisition or estimator was
simulated: the study tests what happens IF those residuals hold. Perfect
calibration examples show the mechanism; combined stress cases use nonzero
residuals. Unknown per-cell phase distortions outside the three-parameter
optical model, amplitude errors, drift and nonuniform hardware quantization
are not covered by these residual settings.

Development screen and finite selection
---------------------------------------
The first screen evaluates x=-6..6 um at 0.25 um spacing, a two-dimensional
625-point alignment grid at 0.5 um spacing, and d=-40..40 nm at 2.5 nm spacing.
Its 512 simultaneous corner settings combine x,y in {-5,+5} um, d in {-30,+30}
nm, gain in {0.98,1.02}, Gaussian phase variance in {0.049,0.051}, background
in {0.10,0.15} counts/gate, position-estimation residuals in {-0.5,+0.5} um
per axis and d-estimation residual in {-5,+5} nm.

The screen propagates independent, UNQUANTIZED Gaussian phasor moments through
order four by a finite exponential-generating-function convolution. With
z_j=w_j*exp(i commanded_mean_j)/sqrt(63), each site's coefficient is
z_j^a*conj(z_j)^b*exp(-s*(a-b)^2/2)/(a! b!). The diagonal coefficient times
(k!)^2 equals E[(I/63)^k]. All 63 sites are included. Independent two-phasor
Gauss-Hermite integration and a closed-form first moment check this recurrence.
Conditional Poisson factorial-moment identities then give the mean and variance
of the fixed count score. Dead time and quantization are omitted ONLY in this
development proxy, and fully restored in the validation simulator.

The signed mean-to-threshold distance divided by sqrt(variance/5000) is used
to rank settings. It is NOT a normal-tail risk estimate or certificate.
The 18-setting first validation plan contains centered and +/-5 um axis cases,
selected adverse corners, two points bracketing each x mean crossing, and an
identical-mask negative control. Its exact selection rule is in study.py.

Before the first validation outcomes were available, select_tolerance.py
screened exactly two tighter residual budgets: (0.25 um,2 nm) and (0.1 um,1 nm).
It chose the first whose minimum corrected standardized margin was at least
four, or the tighter budget if neither qualified. It selected four adverse A
and four adverse B corners for a second fixed plan with a new seed. The first
plan is retained even if it fails. The selector never reads validation files.
There is no further adaptive budget search, threshold fitting or trial rerun.

Finite corners cannot establish extrema of this nonlinear optical model over
an interior box. Passing selected settings does not validate all screened
corners, all parameter combinations, or a continuous alignment neighborhood.
The x mean crossing is not the 5% risk boundary and may be earlier or later
than a practical threshold under a different full detector/noise model.

Held-out trials and statistical interpretation
----------------------------------------------
Each plan uses 200 independent trials per true label at each selected setting,
with 5,000 snapshots per trial. Each snapshot has 63 iid Gaussian phase draws,
ideal uniform quantization, conditional Poisson primaries and exact renewal
sampling of an initially-live nonparalyzable detector (22 ns dead time/5 ms gate).
Different trial indices and labels have different SeedSequences. Separate
detector streams are used per setting and method. Phase draws are shared across
settings and methods within each trial; observations cannot be pooled as iid
across those settings. The two plans use distinct validation seeds.

For each method, setting and label, report the empirical error and two-sided
95% Clopper-Pearson interval. For the corrected non-control family of K error
probabilities within one plan, report one-sided upper limits
BetaQuantile(1-0.05/K, errors+1, trials-errors), or 1 when all trials fail.
Bonferroni supplies at least 95% simultaneous coverage within that specified
family, regardless of cross-setting dependence, under the simulation law.
The gate is that ALL those upper limits must be strictly below 5%.
This gate does not cover the baseline, the control, both plans jointly, new
parameter settings, or the physical apparatus. No trials are discarded.

The identical-mask control programs A for both labels. A successful control
has approximately 50% balanced classification error, not successful separation.
It is reported outside the discrimination success gate.

Limits and measurement handoff
------------------------------
The phase mask is an emulator on a regular grid. The ideal static amplitude
mask is an unvalidated hardware requirement. Scalar/monochromatic propagation,
the Gaussian phase law and iid snapshots remain assumptions. Detector
afterpulsing, pixel crosstalk, real transfer functions, polarization and drift
are not included. Calibration residuals must be measured independently. Neither
more Monte Carlo trials nor the previously passed Lean/Arb proofs establish them.
See LAB_PLAN.txt for a bounded measured-transfer gate and acquisition costs.
