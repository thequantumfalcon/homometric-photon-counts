No observed classification failures at the tested settings.
Three optical cases × eight detector profiles × two labels. Each has 200 trials of 5,000 simulated gates. The one-sided 95% simultaneous upper bound is 3.375% per error probability, across all 48 comparisons.

Three published, processed SPAD afterpulse distributions supply delay shapes. Each detector anchor has a matching recovery-only comparison; an additional offspring mean of 0.05 is an assumed stress case. The optical calibration, phase quantization, photon scaling, score and threshold remain fixed.

This does not establish physical validation. Our optical transfer remains unmeasured. The detector model assumes an initially live detector with no prior traps in every gate and omits twilight pulses, drift and an unmeasured delay tail. Passing three selected optical settings does not certify all settings or a continuous tolerance region. Shared phase draws mean results must not be pooled as independent experiments.
