Optical and counting model
==========================

Let E(x,y)=exp(-(x^2+y^2)/w_in^2) on the 63 square pupil cells. Each cell j
has fixed real amplitude transmission a_j and commanded phase phi_j. The
64th cell is blocked. A signed quadratic phase screen adds exp(2*pi*i*W/lambda).
The ideal lens maps the pupil to the focal plane using the scalar Fraunhofer
transform with POPPY 1.2.0's positive Fourier exponent. Pupil samples are at
cell-centered coordinates. Pixel-integrated complex amplitudes are used so
their squared Euclidean norm equals the sampled power integral.

The normalized collected field mode is
g_f(X,Y)=sqrt(2/pi)/w_f * exp(-((X-delta)^2+Y^2)/w_f^2).
The detector sees the squared coherent mode overlap |<g_f,T E>|^2, not the
intensity at a single camera pixel or a sum over camera pixels. T is the
Fraunhofer operator. The lens focal length and wavelength map its dimensionless
Fourier coordinates into physical micrometers. The chosen finite focal window
contains the Gaussian collection mode; focal and pupil discretizations are
checked separately.

POPPY's adjoint gives coefficients c_j=<T^* g_f,E_j>. Linearity then permits
fast stochastic trials using sum_j a_j c_j exp(i phi_j). The reduction is
checked against an explicitly propagated full pupil image for a random phase
mask, with displaced fibre and a nonzero quadratic screen. It is not an
independent mathematical theorem about the implementation.

An independent continuum calculation uses the analytic pupil representation
of the Gaussian fibre mode. Set w_p=lambda*f/(pi*w_f). With the same sign
convention, the integrand for c_j is

sqrt(2/pi)/w_p * exp(-r^2/w_p^2-r^2/w_in^2)
  * exp(i*2*pi*x*delta/(lambda*f)) * exp(i*2*pi*W/lambda).

Integrate over cell j by tensor Gauss-Legendre quadrature. In the centered,
unaberrated case this also factors into two real Gaussian integrals evaluated
using erf; the two evaluations are separately compared. Midpoint pupil grids
of 64,128,256,512 samples per side are compared with continuum quadrature for
every non-control scenario. These are floating-point convergence diagnostics,
not rigorous interval enclosures. Negative and positive signs are checked.

Define c_min=min_j c_j at the centered continuum reference and a_j=c_min/c_j.
Then 0<a_j<=1 and each nominal a_j c_j=c_min. These transmissions represent an
ideal added amplitude mask. The effective stochastic weights are
w_j=a_j c_j(actual)/c_min, with no re-equalization after misalignment.
The count rate per gate is lambda_count=|sum_j w_j exp(i phi_j)|^2+b.
This fixes the scalar photon budget once at nominal calibration. For reference,
P/c_min^2, where P is the attenuated pupil power integral, is the effective
incident photon budget times scalar detection efficiency needed to make gain
one under the normalized optical model; it is not a measured laser flux.

For each snapshot, phi_j is q*x_G,j plus an independent N(0,s) variate, rounded
to one of 256 uniformly spaced optical phases. Exact symbolic coordinate
metadata is read from the byte-pinned original model. Its decimals are not
treated as exact coordinates. The regular 8x8 optical grid is an emulator:
the research coordinates are encoded as phases, not reproduced as spatial
positions in that grid.

Photon detection uses exact renewal sampling for the specified initially-live
nonparalyzable counter: the first detection waits Exp(lambda_count) in gate
units, and every subsequent accepted event waits dead_fraction plus another
independent Exp(lambda_count). Stop at gate time one. Rejected arrivals during
dead time do not extend it. The check against
P(K>=k)=gammainc(k,lambda_count*(1-(k-1)*dead_fraction))
uses deliberately large dead time to exercise a detectable non-Poisson regime.
At zero dead time the implementation samples Poisson counts directly.
Afterpulsing, event jitter and detector memory across gates are excluded.

The fixed count-only classifier comes from the earlier changed-frequency
study. It observes no latent phase, label or intensity. The new optical
perturbations fall outside the previous certificate's assumptions; no error
bound is inherited automatically. Report all scenarios and Monte Carlo error
intervals, including negative controls and failures. Future measurements must
test the full assumed transfer and noise model before any physical claim.
