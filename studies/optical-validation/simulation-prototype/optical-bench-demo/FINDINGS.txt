Simulation findings, 2026-10-21
===============================

100 independent trials per true label and scenario, 5,000 snapshots per trial.
Fixed score, threshold, photon scaling and modeled amplitude calibration.

Scenario                                  A errors/100   B errors/100
Centered                                             0              0

Fibre +1 um                                          0              0

Identical-mask control (A programmed for both)         0            100

For zero errors among 100 independent trials, the per-hypothesis two-sided
95% Clopper-Pearson interval extends from zero to approximately 3.62%. It is
neither a simultaneous guarantee across this sweep nor a physical error bound.
The identical-mask control has 50% balanced error as expected. Scenarios share
phase draws and cannot be pooled as independent trials. No failed scenario
has been discarded or used to refit the classifier.

Interpretation: the assumed centered optical transfer supports separation
in this simulator, but micron-scale collection errors can break the fixed
classifier. Discrete passing offsets do not certify a continuous tolerance
interval; untested combinations of imperfections may also break separation.
The signed quadratic coefficient is an optical-path parameter, not RMS error.

The next useful step is measured per-cell complex transfer and actual amplitude
mask/phase response, following MEASUREMENT_PLAN.txt. More simulated trials cannot
establish that those assumptions hold in hardware. This demonstration is a
programmed phase-mask emulator, not reconstruction of an unknown specimen.

Numerical consistency: independent Gaussian overlap quadrature, an erf closed
form, grid refinement, forward/adjoint agreement, and a NumPy transform check.
Detector renewal sampling is compared against an analytic waiting-time law.
These are floating-point checks, not new rigorous enclosures or Lean proofs.
REA independently inventories the POPPY wheel; it is not the physics engine.

All original mathematical capsules and published evidence remain unchanged.
The demo is local; no claim of peer review or physical validation is made.
