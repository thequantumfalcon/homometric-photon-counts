Requirements
1. Reconstruct the finite-cell complex Gaussian integrals in Python-FLINT/Arb.
2. Check a closed-form error-function evaluation against validated integration
   of the entire integrand. These are two algorithms sharing the Arb backend,
   not independent implementations of interval arithmetic.
3. Bound differences from the floating-point transfer and correction values.
4. Propagate coefficient errors to intensity errors for fixed unit phasors.
5. Explicitly handle quantizer boundaries in finite saved witness checks;
   do not infer a uniform error guarantee through discontinuous rounding.
6. Store exact dyadic enclosure endpoints, source hashes and reproducible
   scripts. Independently audit algebraic bounds using rational arithmetic.
7. Recheck that previous manifests still bind unchanged files. Save results
   and a handoff; do not replace existing simulation outcomes.

integral algorithms overlap; declared numeric-error caps pass; saved witness

invalid certificate mutations are rejected; standalone artifact audit passes.

or silently changing prior parameters.
