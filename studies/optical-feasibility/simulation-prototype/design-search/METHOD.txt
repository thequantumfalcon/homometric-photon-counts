CONDITIONAL MATHEMATICAL CERTIFICATE FOR THE CHANGED-FREQUENCY DESIGN

1. Model and evidence boundary

Use the two exact coordinate lists from the retained input_model.json, identified
by SHA256 f4f3462b6aac8b56b152f63817cbf5de6848b3226f7dfe26cd29be87490c236f.
Coordinates are reconstructed from integer/offset metadata. The two offsets are
3*asin(1/(5*sqrt(3)))/pi and -3*asin(1/(8*sqrt(3)))/pi. Decimal coordinate strings
are not used as exact data. Let q=201*pi/200, s=1/20, n=63 and

  S = sum_j exp(i*(q*x_j + Z_j)),  I=|S|^2,  J=I/63,

where all Z_j are independent N(0,s). Independent snapshots repeat the entire
random phase draw. Conditional primary counts obey N|I ~ Poisson(I+b).
The gain is fixed at one. Each certificate is conditional on its exact stated
law; measurements have not established that an apparatus follows this law.

2. Mixed-moment calculation

For V_j=exp(i*(q*x_j+Z_j))/sqrt(n), the Gaussian characteristic function gives

  E[V_j^a conjugate(V_j)^b]
   = n^(-(a+b)/2)*exp(i*(a-b)*q*x_j)*exp(-s*(a-b)^2/2).

If M_j(p,r)=E[(sum_{k<=j}V_k)^p*(sum_{k<=j}conjugate(V_k))^r], independence and
two applications of the binomial theorem give

  M_j(p,r)=sum_{a=0}^p sum_{b=0}^r
     binom(p,a)*binom(r,b)*M_{j-1}(p-a,r-b)*E[V_j^a conjugate(V_j)^b].

Initialize M_0(0,0)=1 and all other entries zero. The diagonal M_63(k,k)
is E[J^k]. certify.py evaluates this recurrence for all 63 sites with Arb/acb
enclosures through order five. The exploratory NumPy code instead multiplies
truncated exponential generating functions. Exhaustive two-site expansion and
the retained fourth-gap formula supply separate test oracles.

3. Count moments and the fixed score

For lambda=I+b, expand E[lambda^k] from E[J^j] by the binomial theorem.
For Poisson primaries E[(N)_k]=E[lambda^k]. The elementary identities

  (N)_2^2 = (N)_4+4*(N)_3+2*(N)_2,
  N*(N)_2 = (N)_3+2*(N)_2,  N^2=(N)_2+N

provide E[Y] and E[Y^2] for Y=(N)_2/63^2-(17/10)*N/63. Variance is
E[Y^2]-E[Y]^2. For the separate first-generation afterpulse model, the
conditional factorial generating function is

  E[(1+u)^N | lambda] = exp(lambda*((1+p)*u+p*u^2)),

so its kth factorial moment is

  k! * sum_{j=0}^{floor(k/2)}
     (1+p)^(k-2j)*p^j*E[lambda^(k-j)] / ((k-2j)!*j!).

This model adds at most one child per primary and has no delay or cascading.

4. Finite-sample guarantee

For m iid scores with variance at most V, the variance of their average is
at most V/m. Cantelli's one-sided inequality gives, for a positive distance d
between the hypothesis mean and decision threshold,

  P(classification error) <= V/(V+m*d^2).

For completeness, for any centered variable X with variance v and a>0,
Markov applied to (X+v/a)^2 on {X>=a} yields
P(X>=a) <= (v+(v/a)^2)/(a+v/a)^2 = v/(v+a^2).
Apply this to X or -X as required. The score has finite variance since I is
bounded and Poisson mixtures with bounded rate have all moments.

The code encloses each hypothesis mean, obtains an upper variance bound, fixes
a rational threshold, and takes the smallest distance allowed by the mean
enclosure. The final fraction is evaluated with exact Fraction arithmetic,
using outward rational endpoints exported by Arb. Every reported risk is
strictly below 1/20 at m=5000. The threshold is fixed without inspecting the
held-out simulation. There is no multiple-testing penalty from choosing a
design using deterministic moment calculations and then proving its risk bound.

5. Uniform phase quantization

Let Q_L(x) round a phase to the nearest multiple of 2*pi/L, modulo 2*pi.
For an integer d, the periodic function exp(i*d*Q_L(x)) has Fourier modes
n=d+k*L, with coefficients sinc(n*pi/L). Gaussian convolution therefore gives

  E[exp(i*d*Q_L(theta+Z))]
   = sum_{k in Z} sinc((d+k*L)*pi/L)
       *exp(i*(d+k*L)*theta-s*(d+k*L)^2/2).

The Gaussian factor ensures absolute convergence for s>0. For 0<|d|<L,
write a=L-|d|. Since |sinc|<=1, the absolute sum of all k!=0 terms is at most

  T = 2*exp(-s*a^2/2)/(1-exp(-s*a*L)).

Indeed, for |k|=j>=1, |d+kL|>=a+(j-1)L, whose square is at least
a^2+2*a*L*(j-1); the remaining bound is a geometric series. Add [-T,T]
to both real and imaginary parts of the retained k=0 term. For d=0 the value
is exactly one. Substitute these enclosures for the single-site characteristic
moments in section 2. Here L=256, s=1/20 and |d|<=5; the omitted tail is far
below the 256-bit arithmetic precision, but it is explicitly included.

The bound covers nearest uniform optical phase levels, not an arbitrary
8-bit electro-optical transfer curve. Independent wrapped-normal cell
integration tests the implementation, including a case with visible aliases.

6. Conservative dead-time correction

Scale each 5 ms gate to [0,1]. The 22 ns dead time is d=22/5000000.
Let N count primary arrivals and N' count accepted arrivals for an initially-live
nonparalyzable detector. Let C count unordered primary pairs separated by less
than d. Each rejected event has an earlier accepted event within d, so

  0 <= N-N' <= C.

For any nonnegative function f and a homogeneous conditional Poisson process,
integration over an inserted pair of events gives

  E[f(N)*C | lambda]
    = lambda^2*(d-d^2/2)*E[f(K+2)],  K~Poisson(lambda).

The geometric factor is the measure of 0<u<v<1 with v-u<d. The remaining
Poisson events are independent of the two inserted events. This is the only
point-process identity used beyond the specified arrival law.

Let h=63 and beta=17/10. Direct differences of the quadratic score give

  |Y(N)-Y(N')| <= C*(2*N/h^2+beta/h),
  |Y(N)^2-Y(N')^2|
    <= C*(4*N^3/h^4+6*beta*N^2/h^3+2*beta^2*N/h^2).

The second inequality follows from |Y(k)|<=N^2/h^2+beta*N/h for 0<=k<=N.
Taking expectations with the inserted-pair identity uses rate moments only
through order five and yields nonnegative bounds epsilon1, epsilon2 on the
mean and raw-second-moment changes. These are conservative bounds, not loss
predictions. Known background is included in lambda=I+1/8.

Enclose the accepted-score mean in [mu-epsilon1,mu+epsilon1]. Its raw second
moment is at most E[Y^2]+epsilon2. All resulting mean intervals here lie below
zero; subtract the square of their upper endpoint to bound variance above.
Feed this variance upper bound and widened mean interval into section 4.

The JSON field variance_or_upper_bound_expression records the arithmetic
expression evaluated for this upper bound. Its lower endpoint is NOT a lower
bound on the actual dead-time variance. Only variance_upper_bound_exact is
used for the risk calculation. Afterpulsing, initial recovery and cross-gate
memory are excluded from this dead-time argument.

7. Trust and interpretation

The proof argument and new evaluator have not been formalized in Lean or
externally peer reviewed. The Arb arithmetic implementation and input binding
are trusted components; tests are supplementary checks. No physical noise
calibration, instrument response, practical settling rate, or historical
novelty is established. The certificate concerns the new second-order design
and does not amend the original fourth-order theorem or its fixed-test bounds.
