Adapted mathematical proof code

PhotonPoissonSeries.lean uses the proof of the real exponential/series
identification from mathlib's Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Exponential.lean,
with unused calculus imports avoided. Original source credit: Anatole
Dedecker and Eric Wieser. mathlib is distributed under Apache License 2.0;
the complete license is in LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt.

The public capsule does not bundle Lean, mathlib, FLINT/Arb, GMP, MPFR,
SymEngine, NumPy, or their runtime binaries. Optional separately installed
dependencies remain governed by their own licenses. Scientific citations
in the archived proof notes are references, not claims of ownership of
the referenced research.

External processed SPAD afterpulse distributions

The optical-validation supplement includes apDist_3432H.txt, apDist_3605H.txt
and apDist_Count.txt from Ivo Straka's SPAD-counting-model repository at commit
4ed0903200b59b7f94715c38ca2668bb832e80d8:
https://github.com/ivo-s/SPAD-counting-model/tree/4ed0903200b59b7f94715c38ca2668bb832e80d8
Associated paper: Straka, Grygar, Hlousek and Jezek, Counting Statistics of
Actively Quenched SPADs Under Continuous Illumination,
https://doi.org/10.1109/JLT.2020.2994654.
The upstream GPL-3.0 license is preserved as LICENSE.upstream.txt beside the
data in measured-data-review/data/spad and their copies in afterpulse-study/data,
under studies/optical-validation/simulation-prototype. These unmodified tables
retain their original terms; the project's MIT grant does not replace them.
No upstream executable source or third-party article PDF is distributed here.
