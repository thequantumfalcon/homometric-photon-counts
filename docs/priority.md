## Defensible contribution and priority boundary

The completed formal theorem is a verified result about the explicit model. Historical priority of its precise specialization remains **unestablished**. A comparison must hold the observation model fixed: a higher spatial correlation, a quantum normally ordered correlation across directions, and a raw moment of one random intensity are different data.

The structural quantity selected here is $T=\operatorname{Re}(M_4\overline{M_2}^{\,2})$. It has the same algebraic bispectral form as the phase-sensitive term in Bojer et al.'s equation (16), evaluated at equal arguments. This identification is our algebraic comparison. It neither imports their quantum measurement model nor shows that such arguments are experimentally accessible. The candidate distinction is how this known invariant first enters the specified single-frequency Gaussian experiment, with an exact coefficient and explicit pair.
