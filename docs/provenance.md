# Public evidence provenance

The public capsule is a documented derivative of five retained local research layers. Original sealed local evidence remains separate. The public inventory records both the original and distributed SHA256 for every included scientific file.

Two mechanical transformations were applied: machine-specific workspace/home prefixes became `LOCAL_WORKSPACE` or `LOCAL_USER_HOME`, and dependent file-hash references were rebound transitively. The continuation input manifest was projected onto the selected public files with their current sizes and hashes. This prevents a public input checker from depending on a private path or an omitted inventory member.

The transformation does not change mathematical coefficients, inequalities, root brackets, probabilities, sample counts, or Lean propositions. Public replays and semantic comparison against the retained originals check that boundary. The new hashes identify the public derivative; they must not be represented as the original sealed archive hashes.

Selection excludes private ASTRA source, unrelated desktop inventories, external article PDFs, runtime binaries, compiled proof objects, and local repository settings. The included archived reports may refer to earlier local workflows or omitted rendering artifacts. Current navigation and supported commands are specified in the root documentation.

New formal work is added under `formal/` with a separate source manifest. It does not rewrite historical proof statements or the 327-file public evidence inventory. The mixture construction discharges the tower premises for its constructed laws. The geometry extension checks explicit finite phase expressions and parameter substitution. The [Gaussian extension](../formal/GAUSSIAN.md) proves the general expectation expansion, all-order intensity integrability, and the Gaussian/Poisson marginal construction. The [concrete certificates](../formal/CERTIFICATES.md) now prove the low-order coefficient simplification and final count-law distinction. The [joint snapshot extension](../formal/JOINT_MODEL.md) proves the conditional Poisson interpretation and identifies its marginals. The model-error certificate is a new derived result with its own input/source hashes; it does not alter the inherited probability bounds. The [archive-input bridge](../formal/ARCHIVE_INPUT.md) formally checks parsing of the exact phase-array characters and their algebraic correspondence up to permutation. The [byte-binding receipt](validation/archive_byte_binding.json) separately identifies the unchanged input fragments. The [symbolic-coordinate extension](../formal/COORDINATES.md) now proves the coordinate-to-phase interpretation and full probability-law reindexing. Its separate [coordinate byte receipt](validation/coordinate_byte_binding.json) binds the original metadata arrays to the checked literals. Decimal display text is not used as an exact real input. The earlier exact phasor replay remains a separate computational check. Historical scope files remain accurate descriptions of the earlier modules.

The [quantitative root-stability result](root-stability.md) is another derived result outside the historical evidence tree. Its receipt pins five public reference certificates and the new proof/checker sources. It preserves the earlier existence proof unchanged and adds explicit perturbation bounds; it does not imply that those bounds were present in the sealed archive.

The manifests prove byte identity relative to their anchors, not authenticity against an adversary who replaces both. Signed commits supply a separate provenance layer. GitHub verified the [initial public commit](https://github.com/thequantumfalcon/homometric-photon-counts/commit/d7fece070757c9e045acc554751d286dc928b187) under the maintainer's pseudonymous identity. There is no tagged public release.
