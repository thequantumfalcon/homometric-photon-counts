# Public evidence provenance

The public capsule is a documented derivative of five retained local research layers. Original sealed local evidence remains separate. The public inventory records both the original and distributed SHA256 for every included scientific file.

Two mechanical transformations were applied: machine-specific workspace/home prefixes became `LOCAL_WORKSPACE` or `LOCAL_USER_HOME`, and dependent file-hash references were rebound transitively. The continuation input manifest was projected onto the selected public files with their current sizes and hashes. This prevents a public input checker from depending on a private path or an omitted inventory member.

The transformation does not change mathematical coefficients, inequalities, root brackets, probabilities, sample counts, or Lean propositions. Public replays and semantic comparison against the retained originals check that boundary. The new hashes identify the public derivative; they must not be represented as the original sealed archive hashes.

Selection excludes private ASTRA source, unrelated desktop inventories, external article PDFs, runtime binaries, compiled proof objects, and local repository settings. The included archived reports may refer to earlier local workflows or omitted rendering artifacts. Current navigation and supported commands are specified in the root documentation.

The manifest proves byte identity relative to its anchor, not authenticity against an adversary who replaces both. A future signed commit/release supplies a separate provenance layer. No signed public release is claimed by this candidate.
