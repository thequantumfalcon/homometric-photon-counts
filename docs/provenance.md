The methods disclosure and the reports' independence limitations remain. Dependent hashes and sizes were rebound, and the phase/coordinate byte checks were rerun. The [change record](validation/editorial_cleanup.json) preserves before/after identities; public inventories retain original archive identities and add pre-editorial identities for affected entries. Mathematical model data, numerical results and all Lean source text are unchanged. Historical execution receipts remain historical: updating a hash reference does not claim a new proof execution. The two external byte-binding receipts describe the current distributed input.

The byte-for-byte copying descriptions below refer to initial import, before this recorded editorial transformation.

The public capsule is a documented derivative of five retained local research layers. Original sealed local evidence remains separate. The public inventory records both the original and distributed SHA256 for every included scientific file.

Two mechanical transformations were applied: machine-specific workspace/home prefixes became `LOCAL_WORKSPACE` or `LOCAL_USER_HOME`, and dependent file-hash references were rebound transitively. The continuation input manifest was projected onto the selected public files with their current sizes and hashes. This prevents a public input checker from depending on a private path or an omitted inventory member.

The transformation does not change mathematical coefficients, inequalities, root brackets, probabilities, sample counts, or Lean propositions. Public replays and semantic comparison against the retained originals check that boundary. The new hashes identify the public derivative; they must not be represented as the original sealed archive hashes.

Selection excludes unrelated private source code, desktop inventories, external article PDFs, runtime binaries, compiled proof objects, and local repository settings. The included archived reports may refer to earlier local workflows or omitted rendering artifacts. The [recovery record](report/recovery.json) identifies every restored file. Current navigation and supported commands are specified in the root documentation.
