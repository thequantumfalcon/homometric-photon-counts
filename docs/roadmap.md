# Research roadmap

| Open target | Evidence needed to close it |
| --- | --- |
| Extend formal coverage beyond count-law distinction | Build on the [proved spatial homometry and count-law bridge](../formal/HOMOMETRY.md); formalize the root and testing certificates and their connection to the decision theorem. Instrument diffraction response and graph/covariance claims remain outside this Lean coverage |
| Useful larger root-stability neighborhoods | Improve the [certified rectangle](root-stability.md), whose radii $10^{-23}$ in $t$ and $10^{-20}$ in common background are deliberately conservative; assess tolerances relevant to a specified experiment |
| Global parameter root count | Exclusion of additional roots across all claimed phase widths/backgrounds, not a finite scan |
| Optimal testing and design | Bounds for the exact observation/risk criterion and certified exclusion of competing designs; pooled bins do not automatically bound full counts |
| Historical priority | [Claim-level comparison](priority.md) against the outstanding primary texts, with exact hypotheses and conclusions |
| Physical validation | Numerical optical transfer/SLM calibration and a measured pilot following the [laboratory plan](../studies/optical-validation/simulation-prototype/robustness-study/LAB_PLAN.txt). External processed SPAD delay tables inform conditional simulations; no proposed-apparatus measurements are present |
| Complete numerical optics error budget | Extend the [finite transfer certificate](../studies/optical-validation/README.md) to continuous optical tolerances and stochastic phase-rounding/rate/sampler errors; saved witnesses alone do not supply this bound |
| Broader formal portability | A separately validated Linux/macOS toolchain and dependency build; the supplied replay targets the retained pinned Windows environment |

The current reference four-root count is settled within its analytic/computational assumptions. The [quantitative stability proof](root-stability.md) now supplies an explicit full rectangle, controlling zero and infinite gain as well as the intervals between roots. Its exact rational budget and the fixed-bin necessary count narrow separate gaps. The formal sources now cover concrete phase harmonics, Gaussian expectations, an all-order intensity-moment formula, a proved site-by-site recurrence, and the Gaussian/Poisson marginal count-law construction. The [concrete exact certificates](../formal/CERTIFICATES.md) now close the moment-evaluation gap and prove count-law distinction for the explicit model. The [exact spatial theorem](../formal/HOMOMETRY.md) now proves ordering, distinctness, directed-difference homometry and exclusion of translations/reflections for the saved coordinates. The [joint snapshot model](../formal/JOINT_MODEL.md) also closes the conditional Poisson interpretation; a temporal or exposure-integrated physical model remains a separate extension. Repository polish, licensing, CI, and artifact integrity support inspection but cannot replace missing mathematical or experimental evidence.
