# Research methods and limitations

Substantial parts of the mathematical arguments, code, computational checks, alternative implementations, literature investigation, and documentation were developed with AI assistance. Human direction set the questions, challenged results, and selected work to pursue. The maintainer did not independently derive or manually verify every step.

Successful reproductions and alternative arithmetic implementations are supporting evidence within this workflow. They share some inputs and dependencies and do not constitute external human peer review. The scope of implementation independence is stated in each report.

Lean verification covers the exact checked statements, including their explicit premises. It does not automatically establish the complete geometry-to-detector chain, numerical certificates, historical novelty, or physical adequacy. A disclaimer cannot turn an unproved assertion into a theorem.

The work concerns the stated fixed geometries, phase laws, calibration information, and observation models. It includes no measured detector dataset. Practical feasibility, broader validity, global optimality, and a complete priority comparison remain open. Errors or omissions may remain; concrete criticism and independent reproduction are welcome.

AI systems are not authors or accountable researchers. They are not listed as Git contributors, co-authors, citation authors, or release authors. This methods disclosure documents how the research was produced and does not assert human verification that did not occur.
