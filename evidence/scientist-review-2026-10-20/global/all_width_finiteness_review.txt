Independent review of the qualitative all-width finiteness argument
==================================================================

Reviewed preserved global_blind_gain_proofs.txt and its independent
theory review, alongside the new fixed-t inverse-gain calculation.
No hidden gap was found in the qualitative finite-root argument when
read with its stated fixed positive finite width assumption.

For each fixed width s>0, the wrapped-Gaussian phase density is analytic
on a neighborhood of the compact phase cube. Restricting an analytic
function to such a compact box makes it globally subanalytic; no claim
of a uniform globally subanalytic family over all s>0 is required.
Intensity is analytic, nonconstant, and has a null critical set. The
coarea/absolute-continuity argument therefore produces an absolutely
continuous intensity law. Integrating the bounded globally subanalytic
CDF integrand over its compact phase domain produces a constructible
function by the parameter-integration theorem. Constructible functions
are definable in R_an,exp. Taking the derivative on the differentiability
set preserves definability. In one real variable the exceptional null
set and the boundaries of the positive/negative sign sets are finite.
Thus the density difference has only finitely many sign blocks.

The classical Laplace variation-diminishing argument then bounds the
number of positive zeros of the exponentially tilted fourth-moment
difference by a finite number of sign changes of u^4 times the density
difference. Its proof uses exponential-polynomial Chebyshev systems and
can count multiplicities; it does not require the Laplace transform to
be definable. Nonidentity follows from the already proved nonzero
fourth-moment difference for positive finite width.

Relevant primary theorem statements consulted during the earlier review:
R. Cluckers and D. Miller, Stability under integration of sums of products
of real globally subanalytic functions and their logarithms, Theorem1.3:
https://rcluckers.perso.math.cnrs.fr/prints/MillerStab.pdf
L. van den Dries, A. Macintyre, and D. Marker, The elementary theory of
restricted analytic fields with exponentiation, Corollary5.13:
https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~zchatzid/papiers/o-min-21/DMM.pdf
No network access is needed to replay the new numerical certificates.

This argument proves finiteness separately at each fixed positive finite
width; it supplies neither a uniform finite count across all widths nor
the number four at arbitrary width. The new compactified Fourier proof
settles the exact global count FOUR at t=1/2 only. An exact all-width
count would require additional parameter-uniform sign or variation
control, which has not been supplied by this review.
