New checked results:

PhotonMomentTransfer.affine_fourth_moment proves the actual Bochner-integral
expansion of E[(eta I+b)^4] for a probability measure, given integrability of
the four intensity powers. common_background_cancellation derives
  E_A[(eta I+b)^4] - E_B[(eta J+b)^4]
    = eta^4 (E_A[I^4] - E_B[J^4])
from equality of the first three moments. Both spaces may differ. No
assumption of this final cancellation identity appears in either proof.

PhotonPoissonSeries.factorial_moment_hasSum proves, for every natural k and
real r, the convergent-series identity
  sum_n exp(-r) r^n/n! (n descending-factorial k) = r^k.
Its proof cancels factorials, shifts the exponential series by k, and proves
the omitted prefix is zero. Nonnegative r gives the probability interpretation.
The short real/series-exponential identification follows the existing local
mathlib proof in Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Exponential.lean (Apache-2.0,
Anatole Dedecker and Eric Wieser); unused calculus imports are avoided.

PhotonPoissonMeasure constructs an explicit measure on the natural numbers
as the sum of exp(-r)r^n/n!-weighted Dirac measures, for r in nonnegative reals.
poissonLaw_probability proves normalization. poissonLaw_factorial_integrable
proves integrability for every k. poissonLaw_factorial_moment proves its
factorial integral is r^k. This uses the same defining expression as the local
mathlib Poisson measure, but does not import or claim a checked identification
with the separate mathlib constant.

PhotonMixtureBridge.integrated_factorial_series proves pointwise replacement
of the proved series under an integral. count_gap_and_distinct_laws composes
the actual Poisson factorial-integral theorem with background cancellation.
Given intensity moment matching through order three, the specified fourth
intensity gap D4(exp(-s)), eta>0, s>0, nonnegative rates satisfying
rate=eta I+b, and the two explicit tower identities below, it derives both
  factorialFourth(A)-factorialFourth(B)=eta^4 D4(exp(-s))
and A != B. The prior polynomial positivity result is imported from the
sealed PhotonAlgebra source and recompiled in the isolated replay.

THE REMAINING EXPLICIT TOWER PREMISE, FOR EACH STRUCTURE:
  integral_n (n descending-factorial 4) dA
    = integral_omega [integral_n (n descending-factorial 4)
        d poissonLaw(rate(omega))] dmu.
This is conditional total expectation expressed as integrals. It is NOT
assumed to equal a rate moment or the desired final difference: those steps
are proved here. However, constructing a measurable Poisson kernel/joint law
and deriving this tower identity from that construction remain unformalized.
No Tonelli/interchange proof is claimed by integrated_factorial_series.
