REFERENCE POINTS
---------------
The report correctly distinguishes the parameterizations
t=exp(-s/2) and x=exp(-s)=t^2. The blind-interval and known-calibration
sample certificates use t=1/2, x=1/4, s=2 log 2. The joint-calibration
finite example has the same width and gain, but background b=1/100 rather
than zero. The motion-robustness certificate instead uses s0=log 2,
x=1/2. These reference points and their numerical constants are not
conflated.

GLOBAL CLAIMS
-------------
The detector ordering is stated for ideal Poisson separation per snapshot,
without an unstated physical resource objective. The infinite-gain result
is a supremum, unattained at finite gain; the interior-width result is an
existence theorem, not a computed maximizing width, value, or uniqueness
theorem. The small-noise TV argument retains its normalized amplitude and
rank-two hypotheses. It does not infer TV convergence from weak convergence
to a point mass.

The global blind-gain result is correctly stated as finiteness at each
fixed positive Gaussian variance and zero background. The exactly-one
result is restricted to (0,1/128] at t=1/2. Additional roots above that
interval and the total global integer remain explicitly unresolved.
The report keeps the integration-stability and o-minimality dependencies
separate from the elementary Laplace-transform argument.

CALIBRATION SCOPE
-----------------
The first-three-moment inversion is a global population result within
the stated centered Gaussian phase family and nondegenerate domain.
It does not identify arbitrary phase laws or site-specific noise.
The finite confidence procedure assumes a justified known rate ceiling,
uses one simultaneous event, and may report unresolved. Its guarantee is
unconditional and for a fixed sample size, not conditional-on-selection
confidence or optional-stopping validity.

The m=10^36 certificate is correctly restricted to the stated interior
example, with B=8, K=64, q=32, x=1/4, eta=1/512, b=1/100. The true
calibration is used to certify performance, not supplied to the inference
algorithm. Its conservative sample scale is not described as optimal or
practical. The displayed parameter-width bounds agree with the detailed
certificate. The asymptotic variant explicitly increases both cutoff and
arithmetic resolution; fixed finite-example precision is not presented
as an unlimited-sample implementation.

MOTION AND ROBUSTNESS
--------------------
The report distinguishes dependence between sites from dependence across
snapshots. Projected Gaussian parameters are sufficient distributional
descriptors, not a claim that all covariance entries are identifiable.
The equicorrelation equivalence now explicitly requires centered phases
or P mu=0. The lower-moment counterexamples and the conservative positive
fourth-moment margins match the detailed motion proof. Robust distinction
is not confused with preservation of exact lower-moment equality.
