Conclusion
----------
No mathematical inconsistency or unsupported root/scaling claim was found
in the reviewed snapshot. The report faithfully presents the freshly
established global root counts: two simple positive roots for original and
star, one for harmonic, with signs +,-,+ and +,- respectively. The origin
multiplicities, displayed rounded locations, harmonic long enclosure,
finite-cover partitions, and infinite-tail inequalities match the held
certificates. The following checks are material to that conclusion.

1. Covariance and graph definitions. The threshold graph is explicitly fixed
on the mean positions. The chords (20,22) and (41,43) use zero-based ordinal
indices. C=L^+, tau_G=tr(C), h_i=63*C_ii/tau_G, K_H=63*C/tau_G,
K_0=P*diag(63*h/62)*P and
K_star=P*diag[(63/61)*(h-1/62)]*P agree with the exact model. All have trace
63; v*K therefore has average centered variance v. Star's equality of
diagonals with harmonic is correctly restricted to each configuration.
The report correctly treats identical A/B covariance tau^2*P in the new
homogeneous model as a separate stronger condition.

2. Noise units. In the homogeneous model v=(62/63)*tau^2 and q0=pi/3, so
s=q0^2*tau^2=7*pi^2*v/62 is correct. All sampling powers are explicitly
powers of phase variance s, with fixed detector parameters. The attenuation
x=exp(-s) differs from the nonnegative root coordinate y=7*pi^2*v/62.
The local use of y=I/n^2 in the bounded-witness subsection is separately
defined and does not enter the root formulas. The trace parameter tau_G is
distinguished by its subscript from the homogeneous raw standard deviation
tau; no erroneous identification of these parameters is made.

3. Root transfer and harmonic rates. The report's identity
Delta_star(v)=exp[7*pi^2*v/(62*61)]*D_0(7*pi^2*v/61)
is exact and transfers roots by 61/62, preserving multiplicity and sign.
The harmonic rate 31*(C_ii+C_jj-2*C_ij)/tau_G is correct in coordinate y.
The 2005-term and 203-term group counts, slowest coefficients/rates,
Taylor remainder formulas and derivative-tail lower bound match the
certificate definitions. Strict inequalities in the displayed harmonic
decimal bracket are justified by strict endpoint signs and outward rounding.

4. Scope and correction of the earlier report. The supplement correctly
replaces the former missing-proof status with fresh proofs for the
reconstructed model, without claiming recovery of historical certificate
bytes. It does not claim an exact number of standardized-contrast maxima;
the earlier at-least-three result remains an existence theorem. It keeps
the homogeneous bounded-witness TV lower bound distinct from actual TV,
and its optimum distinct from optimal experimental design. Calibration,
independent observations, physical validation and literature priority
remain explicit limitations. No prior AI agreement or hash is presented as
proof of mathematical correctness.
