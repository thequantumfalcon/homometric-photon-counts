INDEPENDENT EXACT MOMENT RECONSTRUCTION

Scope: both fixed 63-site structures; independent Gaussian site phases at
t=exp(-s/2)=1/2; intensity moments E[I^k], 0 <= k <= 32. This is an exact
computational proof under that model, with a mathematical reduction below.
It is not a proof-assistant formalization or a physical validation.

1. Geometry input and exact pairing

Write alpha=i sqrt(3), r=(1+alpha)/2, r^6=1. The 21-site base in the archive
has, for j=0,...,9, phases
  r^(3j+1) (sqrt(222)+(-1)^j alpha)/15,
  r^(3j)                                  if j=0,1,
  r^(3j) (sqrt(573)-(-1)^j alpha)/24        if j=2,...,9,
and the final site r^30=1.

This equals the following multiset:
  five copies of the pair r*(+sqrt(222)+alpha)/15,
                           r*(-sqrt(222)+alpha)/15;
  four copies of the pair (+sqrt(573)-alpha)/24,
                          (-sqrt(573)-alpha)/24;
  sites +1,+1,-1.
Every phase has modulus one. Structure A is the union of base rotations
0,31,63 (equivalently 0,1,3); B has rotations 0,32,63 (equivalently 0,2,3).
The paired representation follows by separating even and odd j. The separate
verify_arithmetic.py checks equality of the exact 63-element phase multisets
for both geometries in addition to the argument above.

2. Independent product formula

For independent Gaussian phases w_j, E[w_j^a conjugate(w_j)^b]=2^(-(a-b)^2).
For a unit phasor z define the truncated bivariate exponential generating
polynomial
  F_z(X,Y)=sum_{0<=a,b<=P} z^(a-b) 2^(-(a-b)^2) X^a Y^b/(a! b!).
Independence and multiplication of exponential generating functions imply
  E[I^k]=(k!)^2 [X^k Y^k] product_j F_zj(X,Y).
Every exponent is nonnegative, so discarding terms with either exponent
greater than P after any intermediate multiplication does not affect any
requested coefficient. No cumulants, moment-to-cumulant recurrence, saved
power sums, or saved higher moment coefficients are used.

3. Eliminate the extra radicals before long products

Within a pair let c=sqrt(222) or sqrt(573). Decompose the first site factor
as U+c V, where U,V have coefficients in Q(alpha). Its paired site replaces
c by -c and has factor U-c V. Their product is U^2-c^2 V^2 and belongs to
Q(alpha). Raising the pair factors to powers five and four, including the
three real sites, and rotating by sixth roots of unity therefore requires
only arithmetic in Q(alpha), alpha^2=-3. A rotation by r^h multiplies the
(a,b) coefficient by r^(h(a-b)).

4. Integer scaling: no floating point and no denominator approximations

Set L=lcm(1,...,P) and H=120 L 2^P. Substitute X=H x,Y=H y.
For an unrotated site z let w=120 z. The coefficient becomes the vector
for w^|a-b| (complex-conjugated for a<b), multiplied by
  120^(a+b-|a-b|) L^(a+b)/(a! b!) * 2^(P(a+b)-(a-b)^2).
All factors are integers: a! divides L^a, b! divides L^b, and
(a-b)^2 <= P |a-b| <= P(a+b). Arithmetic in the pair fields has integral
structure constants alpha^2=-3 and c^2 in {222,573}. Exact rotation divisions
by 2 are checked. The final moments are the diagonal integer coefficients
times (k!)^2/H^(2k), reduced with Python Fraction. The alpha coefficient on
every diagonal is required to be exactly zero.

5. Deterministic signed Kronecker multiplication

For rectangular polynomials with exponents through P, substitute y=x^(2P+1)
and x=B, where B is a power of 256. A product has x exponent at most 2P,
so this stride does not identify different bivariate product coefficients.
Let m_A,m_B be the largest absolute real/alpha integer coefficients of the
two inputs. A product coefficient is bounded in absolute value by
  C=4(P+1)^2 m_A m_B,
because real multiplication is ac-3bd and imaginary multiplication is ad+bc,
and at most (P+1)^2 pairs contribute to any bivariate coefficient. Choose
B/2>C. This covers every product coefficient, including discarded ones.
Each integer multiplication is exact. Signed base-B coefficients are decoded
by adding the integer whose every digit is B/2 and subtracting B/2 from each
decoded digit. Since |coefficient|<B/2, the shifted digits are in [0,B), so
there is no carry ambiguity. This is deterministic exact arithmetic; there
is no modular-probability argument, floating-point FFT, or tolerance test.

6. Dependency boundary and reproducibility

reconstruct.py imports only the Python standard library. It opens the saved
fixed_t_moments.json only AFTER constructing every moment for both structures.
All comparisons use exact rational equality. The result records source and
input hashes, per-multiplication bounds, timings, all reconstructed rational
values and individual comparisons. verify_arithmetic.py compares the packed
convolution with an ordinary four-loop convolution on signed coefficients,
including 129-bit values, and verifies the 63-site multiset reductions.

Commands from the workspace root:
  python audit-2026-10-20/moments/run_audit.py --rebuild
To verify existing reconstructed artifacts without the approximately eight-
minute high-order rebuild, omit --rebuild. This wrapper is the supported
audit entry point: it rejects -O, -OO, and PYTHONOPTIMIZE modes that disable
the producer's internal assertions. It also checks source/input hashes and
performs an explicit-raise exact comparison independent of all_equal flags.
Raw component scripts must be run with ordinary, nonoptimized Python.

This closes the specific previously stated dependency gap of independently
rebuilding the full order-32 table from geometry. It does not by itself prove
the downstream analytic inequalities: those still require their documented
proof arguments and certificate replays. It also does not settle the unknown
global optimizer, exact global root count, exact optimal sample integer,
physical validity, external peer review, or priority over all prior work.

7. Additional all-t identity certificate

symbolic_fourth.py uses the same independent site-product construction with
the Gaussian characteristic moment t^((a-b)^2), evaluated exactly at the
33 distinct integer nodes t=1,...,33. Values above one are formal polynomial
evaluations; they are not claimed to represent Gaussian distributions.

Every term contributing to E[I^k] allocates nonnegative a_j,b_j with
sum_j a_j=sum_j b_j=k. Its power of t is
  sum_j (a_j-b_j)^2
  <= sum_j a_j^2 + sum_j b_j^2
  <= (sum_j a_j)^2 + (sum_j b_j)^2 = 2k^2.
Thus the fourth-moment polynomial has degree at most 32, and lower moments
have smaller degree bounds. At every one of the 33 nodes the exact product
calculation establishes D_0=D_1=D_2=D_3=0 and
  D_4(t)=455015761/20000 * t^8 (1-t^2)^5 (t^6+3t^4+6t^2+6).
The target has degree 24. A polynomial of degree at most 32 that vanishes
at 33 distinct nodes is identically zero. These are therefore identities
for every t, not numerical samples or a grid-based approximation.
All displayed factors of D_4 are strictly positive for 0<t<1; this is exactly
every finite positive Gaussian phase variance under t=exp(-s/2).

The all-t verifier does not open the archive moment table. It records all
33 exact evaluations in symbolic_fourth.json. Its scaling H=240 L q^P for
t=p/q uses the additional integer factor p^((a-b)^2) and an exact denominator
factor q^(P(a+b)-(a-b)^2), so all intermediate computations remain integral.

8. Deliberate corruption test

compare_moments.py checks every required rational coefficient explicitly and
raises an error on mismatch. mutation_check.py runs that checker on the
original table, then on a separate copy with one added to B's 32nd moment.
It requires success on the original and failure at the modified entry,
and checks that the archived bytes were unchanged. The intentionally false
copy is named corrupted_moment_fixture.json and is test data, not evidence.
