Checks performed
================
1. Amplitude increment identity: stationarity gives zero mean for every
W_j(u)-W_j(0), and independence across sites kills the cross terms even
though clean phasors are complex. Gaussian increment variance is
2s(1-exp(-u/tau)), giving exactly
2n[1-exp(-s(1-exp(-u/tau)))] for E|F(u)-F(0)|^2.

2. The clean null gives E|F|^2=n(1-exp(-s)). Cauchy-Schwarz uses
E(|F(u)|+|F(0)|)^2<=4n(1-exp(-s)). Combining these quantities gives
2sqrt(2)n times the recorded square root. Integrating sqrt(u/tau) over
[0,D] and dividing by D contributes 2/3; C=4sqrt(2)n/3 times
sqrt(s(1-exp(-s))) is correct.

3. The Poisson additive coupling gives probability of discrepancy
1-exp(-|lambda-mu|), bounded by the rate difference. The shared path
couples the time-average and snapshot intensity. Background cancels in
their rate difference. The product TV inequality requires independent
observations and is valid under the explicit fresh-trajectory reset model.

4. The total-per-gate normalization eta J_D is clearly specified. An
ordinary fixed physical rate would yield eta proportional to D. The proof
does not conflate those cases or assert feasibility of keeping eta fixed.

5. The archived score selects A iff the score sum is nonnegative. Its
exponential-moment inequalities and independence yield conditional error
at most exp(-md) under both models, including the prescribed tie convention.
With m=ceil(6/d) this is at most exp(-6)<1/400. The verifier's integer
ceiling formula and exact inequalities are correct.

6. The positive atanh series remainder bound for log(2) is valid. It gives
2log(2)<7/5, and C^2<(32/9)*63^2*(21/20)=74088/5. Squaring the positive
TV condition gives the stated exposure ratio. The exact final margin
1/400+1/25=17/400<1/20 is valid.
