INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE CONTINUOUS-EXPOSURE EXTENSION

Method: internal AI mathematical cross-check performed separately from the
derivation; not external peer review or proof-assistant checking.

Reviewed continuous_exposure_proof.txt and verify_continuous_exposure.py.
Replayed the exact verifier successfully on 20 October 2026 local date.

The essential dependence step is valid. For disjoint OU path segments,
conditioning the earlier segment on its final state and the later segment
on its initial state reduces their covariance to a stationary OU transition.
The contraction factor is exp(-(kH-D)/tau); it is one for touching gates.
Conditional expectations contract centered L2 norms. Conditional Poisson
independence removes cross-gate emission covariance. Thus the use of the
marginal score variance as an upper bound is conservative and valid.

Summing the geometric covariance bounds gives exactly
1+2 exp(D/tau)/(exp(H/tau)-1), with no independence assumption on gates.
The finite-exposure coupling is a marginal argument and survives replacing
independent resets by one stationary path. The total gate gain is fixed;
no claim about fixed flux as D tends to zero is implied.

The score expectation bounds use the sum of probabilities equal to one,
and subtract the fourth score before interval evaluation. This preserves
the cancellation needed here. Subtracting the minimum square over the
certified mean interval from the second-moment upper bound is a valid
variance upper bound. The same TV allowance transfers both score and
squared-score expectations using their oscillations.

The deterministic threshold lies strictly between the two exposed mean
intervals. Cantelli bounds each conditional error, including the stated tie
rule. Taking floor(19*kappa*V/d0^2)+1 enforces the strict error target <1/20.
Both rational bounds for kappa and the elapsed-span calculations check out.

Result: no mathematical or arithmetic defect found in this extension.
Its limits remain material: known stationary independent-site OU motion,
known parameters, conditional independent emissions, fixed total gate gain,
very small gate width and very large sufficient acquisition times. It
supplies a conditional theorem, not practical feasibility or optimality.
