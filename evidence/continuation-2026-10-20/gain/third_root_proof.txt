THIRD SIMPLE FOUR-COUNT BLIND GAIN

Fix the archive's two 63-site structures, independent Gaussian phases with
s=2 log(2), t=1/2, zero detector background, and positive total gain eta.
Let I be intensity, supported in [0,3969], and define

 F(eta)=E_A[I^4 exp(-eta I)]-E_B[I^4 exp(-eta I)].

The difference of probabilities of exactly four counts is eta^4 F(eta)/24.
Thus its positive zeros, multiplicities, and signs coincide with those of F.
Bounded support allows differentiation under the expectation at every eta.

New input

The continuation reconstructs moments through order64 directly from the
site generating-function product, using exact integer multiplication.
All66 moment values through order32 agree exactly with the sealed earlier
reconstruction. The higher64 values are new output of the same proven
construction. Native integer arithmetic is an explicit computational
dependency, not a machine-code formal proof.

Optional high-precision orthogonal-polynomial quadrature suggests rational
interpolation nodes. Their approximate origin does not enter validity.
The certificate verifies that they are distinct and inside the support,
and uses exact rational Hermite weights and exponential enclosures only.

For exp(-eta x), interpolation at n positive double nodes has error of
sign positive, since its 2n-th derivative is positive and the node product
is a square. Adding a simple node at0 makes the error nonpositive on the
support, since the next derivative is negative and x>=0. Integrating
against x^r dP produces lower and upper bounds using moments through
r+2n-1 or r+2n. No claim that the suggested nodes are exact Gaussian nodes
is required. This is the same Hermite remainder argument used in the
preserved archive, now with more exact moments.

Combining structure bounds gives lower/upper bounds for F and for
F'=E_B[I^5 exp(-eta I)]-E_A[I^5 exp(-eta I)].

Interval exclusion and the new root

The new producer proves, with exact rational Taylor/Bernstein bounds:

  F>0  on [1/32,1/23]          (2 interval leaves),
  F'<0 on [1/23,1/22]          (1 interval leaf),
  F<0  on [1/22,1/12]          (5 interval leaves).

Continuity and the endpoint signs give a root in (1/23,1/22). Strictly
negative derivative makes it unique there and simple. The adjacent covers
exclude all other roots between1/32 and1/12. Combining this with the
archive's exactly two simple roots on (0,1/32] gives exactly THREE simple
roots on (0,1/12]. This counts roots everywhere on the interval, not merely
sign changes on a finite grid.

Independent verification

independent_order64.py uses the archived confluent Newton interpolation
implementation, positive exponential series followed by reciprocation,
and interval Horner covers. This differs from the producer's cardinal
Hermite basis, alternating exponential series, and Bernstein bounds.
It checks the same exact moment input; it is not a second derivation of
the moments from geometry. Independent covers and point checks are saved
beside the producer certificate.

Scope

The previously proved universal four-sign-change theorem implies at least
one additional sign-changing root above1/12. The new finite-interval result
does not prove an exact global count or an effective last-root cutoff.
At a blind gain only the four-count probability agrees: the full count
laws need not agree. No experimental or external-review claim is made.
